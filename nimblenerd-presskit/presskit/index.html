Shape the Way

A calm, clever puzzle game.

Download press copy ↓ View screenshots ↘ Press enquiries ↗

Description

Place the shapes. Drop the ball. Reach the goal.

Shape the Way is a minimalist physics puzzle game built around simple interactions, carefully composed levels and the satisfaction of discovering the right arrangement.

Place and rotate shapes to guide a ball towards a star. Each puzzle has one intended solution to uncover, encouraging players to experiment, rethink their approach and enjoy the moment when everything clicks into place.

Shape the Way

Discover a collection of 242 carefully composed physics puzzles. Every shape has a purpose. Place and rotate them to find the right arrangement, then drop the ball and watch it make its way to the star.

Simple ideas gradually develop into more imaginative combinations of shapes, movement and timing. A restrained visual style, carefully considered controls and calm audio keep the focus on the puzzles.

Features

  • 242 carefully composed puzzles
  • Minimalist design built around clarity, colour and movement
  • Simple, precise controls with layouts to suit how you play
  • Unlimited hints — reveal the placement of one shape at a time
  • Customisable puzzle colours
  • Keep progress in sync with iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac
  • Play every puzzle offline
  • Calm music and subtle sound effects

About NimbleNerd Games

NimbleNerd Games is an independent game developer based in the United Kingdom. Its work focuses on distinctive visual design, playful ideas and approachable experiences built with care.

Shape the Way continues the studio’s interest in thoughtful, physics-based puzzles, following earlier work on Solve & Roll.
