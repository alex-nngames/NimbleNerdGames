SHORT DESCRIPTION
Shape the Way is a calm, clever physics puzzle game about placing and rotating shapes, then dropping a ball to guide it towards a star.

EXTENDED DESCRIPTION
Discover a collection of 242 carefully composed physics puzzles. Every shape has a purpose. Place and rotate them to find the right arrangement, then drop the ball and watch it make its way to the star.

Each puzzle has one intended solution to uncover. Experiment, rethink your approach, and enjoy the moment when the solution finally becomes clear.

Simple ideas gradually develop into more imaginative combinations of shapes, movement and timing.

FEATURES
- 242 carefully composed puzzles
- Minimalist design built around clarity, colour and movement
- Simple, precise controls with layouts to suit how you play
- Unlimited hints — reveal the placement of one shape at a time
- Customisable puzzle colours
- Keep your progress in sync with iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac
- Play every puzzle offline
- Calm music and subtle sound effects
